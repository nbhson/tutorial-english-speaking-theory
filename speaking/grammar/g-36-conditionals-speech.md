# Grammar 36 — Conditionals in speech (If I were you, I would have…)

> **Nhóm F: GIAO TIẾP & PHẢN XẠ**

---

## I. CẤU TRÚC

| Loại | If-clause | Main clause | Ví dụ |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| 0 (sự thật) | If + hiện tại | hiện tại | If it rains, I stay home. |
| 1 (có thể) | If + hiện tại | will + V | If it rains, I will stay home. |
| 2 (giả định) | If + quá khứ đơn | would + V | If I were you, I would go. |
| 3 (quá khứ) | If + had + V3 | would have + V3 | If I had known, I would have called. |

**Quy tắc:** Trong văn nói, type 2 và 3 phổ biến nhất (khuyên, hối tiếc).

## II. KHI NÀO DÙNG?

- **Khuyên**: *If I were you, I would…*
- **Hối tiếc quá khứ**: *If I had known, I would have…*
- **Giả định hiện tại**: *If I had money, I would buy it.*
- **Kết quả có thể**: *If you study, you will pass.*

### ⚠️ Lỗi thường gặp
- ~~If I would have known~~ → *If I **had** known* (Type 3) — không dùng "would have" trong mệnh đề if.
- ~~If I was rich, I would buy~~ → formal: *If I **were** rich…* (subjunctive) — "was" vẫn chấp nhận khi nói thân mật.
- ~~If you study, you would pass~~ → *If you study, you **will** pass* (Type 1) — hiện tại dùng "will", không "would".

## III. NGỮ PHÁP CHI TIẾT

### Cách dùng 1: Type 2 (khuyên / giả định hiện tại)
**Phản xạ hỏi–đáp:**
- ❓ Hỏi: `What should I do?` *(Tôi nên làm gì?)*
- ➕ Đáp: *If I were you, I would talk to her.* — *Nếu tôi là bạn, tôi sẽ nói chuyện với cô ấy.*
- 💬 Đáp lại: *Good idea.*

**Ví dụ:**
1. If I were rich, I would travel the world. — *Nếu tôi giàu, tôi sẽ đi khắp thế giới.*
2. If I had time, I would help you. — *Nếu tôi có thời gian, tôi sẽ giúp bạn.*
3. I would go if I could. — *Tôi sẽ đi nếu có thể.*
4. If she knew the truth, she would tell everyone. — *Nếu cô ấy biết sự thật, cô ấy sẽ nói với tất cả.*
5. What would you do if you found a wallet on the street? — *Bạn sẽ làm gì nếu nhặt được ví trên đường?*

### Cách dùng 2: Type 3 (hối tiếc quá khứ)
**Ví dụ:**
1. If I had known, I would have called you. — *Nếu tôi biết, tôi đã gọi bạn.*
2. If she had studied, she would have passed. — *Nếu cô ấy học, cô ấy đã đậu.*
3. I would have helped if you had asked. — *Tôi đã giúp nếu bạn hỏi.*
4. If we had left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight. — *Nếu chúng tôi đi sớm hơn, chúng tôi đã không lỡ máy bay.*
5. I would have bought it if it had been cheaper. — *Tôi đã mua nếu rẻ hơn.*

### Cách dùng 3: Mixed conditional
**Ví dụ:**
1. If I had studied (quá khứ), I would be rich now (hiện tại). — *Nếu tôi đã học, giờ tôi đã giàu.*
2. If I were taller (hiện tại), I would have played basketball (quá khứ). — *Nếu tôi cao hơn, tôi đã chơi bóng rổ.*
3. If she had taken the job, she would be at home now. — *Nếu cô ấy nhận việc, giờ cô ấy đã ở nhà.*
4. If I hadn't met you, I would still be living in Hanoi. — *Nếu tôi không gặp bạn, tôi vẫn sống ở Hà Nội.*
5. If you were more careful, you wouldn't have broken it. — *Nếu bạn cẩn thận hơn, bạn đã không làm vỡ.*

**Biến tấu (tự luyện):**
- Đổi chủ ngữ: *If I were you, I would go.* → *If she were you, she would go.*
- Đổi thì: *If I had known, I would have called.* → *If I knew, I would call.*
- Đổi ngữ cảnh (đi làm): *If I were the boss, I would approve it.* / (du lịch): *If I had time, I would visit Paris.* / (mua sắm): *If it were cheaper, I would buy it.*

## IV. BẢNG CỤM TỪ GIAO TIẾP

| # | Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
|---|--------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | If I were you… | Nếu tôi là bạn… | Khuyên |
| 2 | I would have… | Tôi đã sẽ… | Hối tiếc |
| 3 | If only… | Giá mà… | Ước muốn |
| 4 | Suppose / Supposing… | Giả sử… | Giả định |
| 5 | Unless… | Trừ khi… | Điều kiện phủ định |

## VI. LUYỆN NÓI

1. If I were you, I would apologize.
2. If I had known, I would have called.
3. If only I had more time.
4. Unless it rains, we'll go.
5. I would have helped if you asked.

**5 câu hỏi tự luyện:**
1. What would you do? — *If I were you, I would…*
2. Why didn't you call? — *If I had known, I would have…*
3. Do you regret it? — *If only I had…*
4. Will you go? — *Unless it rains, yes.*
5. What if he comes? — *If he comes, I'll leave.*

**Ngữ cảnh thực tế:**
- Đi làm: *If I were you, I'd talk to the manager.* / *If I had known, I'd have prepared.* / *If we finish early, we can leave.* / *If it rains, we'll cancel.*
- Du lịch: *If I had more time, I'd visit the museum.* / *If it were cheaper, I'd book it.* / *If you get lost, call me.* / *If the weather is bad, we'll stay in.*
- Mua sắm: *If it were on sale, I'd buy two.* / *If I had cash, I'd pay now.* / *If you don't like it, return it.* / *If it's too expensive, don't buy.*

**Ví dụ thực tế thêm (6 câu):**
1. If I were you, I would apologize. — *Nếu tôi là bạn, tôi sẽ xin lỗi.*
2. If I had known, I would have called. — *Nếu tôi biết, tôi đã gọi.*
3. If only I had more time. — *Giá mà tôi có thêm thời gian.*
4. Unless it rains, we'll go. — *Trừ khi mưa, chúng ta sẽ đi.*
5. I would have helped if you asked. — *Tôi đã giúp nếu bạn hỏi.*
6. What if he comes? — *Nếu anh ấy đến thì sao?*

---

## VII. BONUS: LỖI HAY GẶP

- ❌ *If I was you…* → ✅ *If I **were** you…* (giả định dùng were cho mọi ngôi)
- ❌ *If I would have known…* → ✅ *If I **had** known…* (if-clause không dùng would)
- ❌ *I would go if I will have time.* → ✅ *I would go if I **had** time.*
- 💡 Mẹo: Trong văn nói, "If I were you" là cụm khuyên phổ biến nhất. Dùng "If only…" để than thở/hối tiếc. "Unless" = "if not" — ngắn gọn hơn.
